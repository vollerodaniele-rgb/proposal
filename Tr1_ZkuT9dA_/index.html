What a reel means here

Ten reels means ten finished pieces, not ten single unbroken takes. A long continuous take is used when it earns its place, to honour a treatment done well or to let a specialist finish a thought without a cut.

Clients on camera

Nobody is filmed without written permission, and no treatment is shown in a way that identifies a client unless they have agreed to it in writing. We bring the release forms and handle them on the day, so your team does not have to.
